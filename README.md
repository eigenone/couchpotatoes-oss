# CouchPotatoes 🥔

**An open source engine, UX toolkit and framework for building digital board games and card games.**

🌐 **Website:** https://couchpotatoes.eigen1.com

CouchPotatoes is an open source project that gives back to the community of gamers and developers who want to bring new board game and card game ideas to life. We provide the underlying game engine, user experience components and frameworks so you can spend your time on what makes your game unique, not on rebuilding turn order, card decks and lobby screens from scratch.

## Why CouchPotatoes?

Building a digital tabletop game usually means solving the same problems over and over: managing game state, enforcing rules, handling turns, shuffling and dealing, syncing players, and building a UI that feels good to play. CouchPotatoes takes care of that groundwork so you can go from idea to playable prototype quickly.

## What we provide

- **Game engine**: core primitives for game state, turns and phases, rules validation, randomness (dice, shuffles), and player actions.
- **UX components**: reusable, accessible building blocks for boards, cards, hands, decks, tokens, score tracking, lobbies and game flow.
- **Frameworks**: opinionated patterns and tooling for structuring a game, testing rules, and supporting local and online multiplayer.
- **Examples and templates**: starter projects you can fork to try a new idea.

## Who it's for

- **Game designers** who want to prototype and playtest digital versions of their designs.
- **Developers** who want to build board and card games without reinventing the infrastructure.
- **Players and hobbyists** who want to tinker, mod and contribute to games they love.

## How we develop

CouchPotatoes is developed in the open:

- **Public roadmap and discussions**: planning, design proposals and feature requests happen in GitHub Issues and Discussions.
- **Community-driven**: anyone can propose ideas, report bugs, or contribute code, art, docs and examples.
- **Small, reviewable changes**: work lands through pull requests that are reviewed by maintainers and the community.
- **Modular by design**: the engine, UX components and frameworks are kept loosely coupled so you can adopt only the pieces you need.
- **Original games only**: our examples and templates showcase original designs and public-domain classics, and we respect the intellectual property of existing games.

## Project status

🚧 CouchPotatoes is in its early stages. The architecture and first components are being shaped now, which makes this a great time to get involved and influence the direction of the project.
